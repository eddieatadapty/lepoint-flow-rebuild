Day 1

Pass the campaign and keyword into Adapty

After a one-time SDK setup, the Meta campaign or the Apple Ads keyword each install came from arrives as a user attribute and picks the flow at first open. No app release.

Week 1

One flow per promise

The interview, the live feed and the weekly each get their own first screens; your brand search “le point” keeps today’s first launch for people who already read you.

Week 2

Judge each source on what it earns

Each flow against today’s, measured on trials, paid subscriptions and what each install from that ad or search earns, so spend follows the promise that pays.

All three phones run the whole flow with the five ideas. Your Black Week ads (“-70% sur Le Point : notre plus belle offre de l’année, jusqu’au 30 novembre.”) are the biggest push in the Meta Ad Library right now, and the ones that show a link go to abo.lepoint.fr; the app’s public prices carry no such offer. On Apple Ads you hold about 97% of US searches for “le point”, a strong brand defence; some high shares are radio searches, like “rfi” (84%) and “rfi pure radio” (82%): worth checking what those installs earn. The search data is account-level and US only. This layer carries no range: only a test per ad can give one.
